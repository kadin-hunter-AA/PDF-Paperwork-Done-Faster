# PDF letter feather studies

15 concept variations of quill-pdf-letter-feather-v3.png. The purple PDF letter strokes must form the feather itself, with transparent counters and gaps, rather than lettering inside an ordinary feather. Preserve broad square-icon balance, purple/lavender color, gold trim/shaft and detailed gold nib. No paper.

Open gallery.html to compare on light/dark surfaces. Built-in image_gen prompts are in PROMPTS.json. These are draft PNG masters; no final ICO or installer integration is implied. Production work must clean alpha edges, control exact brand colors and optically adjust small sizes.
